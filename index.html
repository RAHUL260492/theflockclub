What is The Flock Club

Your people are out here — we just help you find them.

The Flock Club is a community built around two simple things: feeling well, and feeling connected. We host warm, in-person experiences — from easy morning runs to soulful music nights — where you can move, breathe, and actually meet people. No fitting a mold, no pretending. Come as you are; leave with a flock.

What we do

Two ways in. One flock.

The Anchor

Runs, walks & a really good coffee after.

Our weekly easy-in: a community run or walk through the park, followed by a social. Show up at any pace, stay for the conversation. It’s how most of the flock first meets.

Weekly Free / low-cost All paces welcome

The Flagship

A soulful music & wellness gathering, once a month.

Our signature night: live acoustic, Sufi & devotional-meets-modern music, guided breathwork or meditation, and a warm, open crowd. A reset for the senses — and the soul.

Monthly Ticketed Live music + breathwork
